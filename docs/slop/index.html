Everything you need to disrupt the safe area

We asked ourselves: what if the bottom 64 points of your app could feel alive?

🎵

Popup Bars, Reimagined™

Floating, compact, glass. Docked to your tab bar, your toolbar, or the raw void at the bottom of the screen. Swipe up to present, swipe down to dismiss, tap the close button to feel something.

🫧

Minimization, but make it liquid

The bar shrinks when you scroll. It's not hiding — it's practicing mindfulness.

↔️

Full Right-to-Left

Hebrew, Arabic, and every other language that reads the correct way around. Our popup bar is fluent in both directions — it's not just localized, it's emotionally bilingual.

📱

iPhone Duo™ Ready

Two screens. One popup bar. Fully supported when targeting the iOS 27.1 SDK and later. We folded so your layout doesn't have to.

🖱️

Pointer Interactions

Hover your iPad cursor over the bar and it glows. Like this website, but tasteful.

🧠

Popup Item Paging

Swipe sideways on the bar to move between items with a data source. Your users will think it's magic. It's LNPopupItem.

⚡

ProMotion at 120Hz

Every frame of the transition is rendered at the speed of thought. Thought, in this case, being 120 times per second.

Heads up: for SwiftUI, there's LNPopupUI. It's not a different library — it's the same journey, declaratively.
Pro tip: present the popup bar on the outermost container controller. The README has said this since 2015. Nobody reads the README. You're reading a landing page instead. We get it.
🔌 ZERO-CONFIG*

Ship a mini-player in three lines

Our proprietary presentPopupBar engine leverages cutting-edge Objective-C categories to synergize your tab bar controller with your content controller.

  • ✅ Swift Package Manager
  • ✅ Swift and Objective-C
  • ✅ iOS, iPadOS, Mac Catalyst
  • ✅ Safe area insets maintained, like a responsible adult

*Config is optional. Config is also extensive. See the README's 30-item table of contents.

NowPlaying.swift 
import LNPopupController

nowPlaying.popupItem.title = "Disrupting the Safe Area"
nowPlaying.popupItem.subtitle = "Dr. Sarah Chen ft. UITabBar"
tabBarController?.presentPopupBar(with: nowPlaying)
